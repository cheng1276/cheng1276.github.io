## 5. Notes for the editor (not row errors)

1. **ACR 2022 PMC extraction:** the PMC text drops "against" in three statements: other diets, electrotherapy and chiropractic. It also drops "no" in the orthoses evidence sentence. The website rows RA-3.2 and RA-M4 already state the correct direction ("不建議"). Electrotherapy is not on the page.
2. **The evidence file needs a correction.** Under RA-C59 / RA-5.2, EULAR fatigue Rec 2 is LoE 1a, GoR A, LoA 9.6 (1.0), and Rec 3 is 1a/A, 9.5 (1.2). Source: UWE accepted manuscript, Table 1.
3. **Currency of the axSpA sources.**
   - The ACR/SAA/SPARTAN 2015 non-drug recommendations used here are still in force. The 2019 update (PMC6764882) states: "The 2015 recommendations that were not re-examined are to be considered extant."
   - An ACR/SAA/SPARTAN 2026 update was approved on 31 May 2026. Only a summary PDF exists so far; the summary says the full manuscripts "will be submitted for publication", and no full paper was found in PubMed on 2026-10-06.
   - The summary is consistent with the website's direction, but changes two strengths:
     - Fall evaluation becomes **strong** (it is conditional in 2015). This affects the AS-3.4 label.
     - Spinal manipulation becomes **strongly against** with no population restriction (in 2015 it applied only to fusion or advanced osteoporosis). This affects AS-3.2.
   - Unsupervised back exercises and active-over-passive physical therapy stay conditional, and active uveitis becomes a good-practice statement for co-management with an ophthalmologist.
   - No website statement becomes unsafe, but re-label AS-3.2 and AS-3.4 when the full guideline is published.
4. **Alcohol amounts:** EULAR 2021 does not quantify "low" or "moderate" alcohol. Do not add numbers to RA-4.4.
5. **Fall urgency wording (AS-3.3, AS-S2):** none of the cited sources gives a time frame for seeking care after a fall. Any "立即/急診" wording is the rheumatologist's own clinical advice and should be marked EDITORIAL.
