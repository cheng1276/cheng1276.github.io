  - Wedged insoles: conditional against. TENS: strong against. Glucosamine: strong against. Fish oil and vitamin D: conditional against.
  - All five are printed with "against" in the typeset co-published PDF, and two independent reads agree:
    - "Lateral and medial wedged insoles are conditionally recommended against in patients with knee and/or hip OA."
    - "Transcutaneous electrical stimulation (TENS) is strongly recommended against in patients with knee and/or hip OA."
    - "Glucosamine is strongly recommended against in patients with knee, hip, and/or hand OA."
    - "Fish oil is conditionally recommended against in patients with knee, hip, and/or hand OA."
    - "Vitamin D is conditionally recommended against in patients with knee, hip, and/or hand OA."
  - The AAFP summary agrees for all five ("Treatments Recommended Against" / "CONDITIONALLY RECOMMENDED AGAINST"). NCCIH agrees for glucosamine.
  - Every "for" direction on the page (exercise, weight loss, cane, braces, CBT, yoga, heat/cold, paraffin) is confirmed by the typeset PDF and/or the PubMed abstract's list of strong and conditional recommendations.
- **Corrected verbatim quotes** for the public toggle are given in the OA-2.2, OA-6.4, OA-6.5, OA-M1 and OA-M3 rows. After the swap, the "PMC drops 'against'" explanation in each strength field can be shortened to "Conditional/Strong against (2019)".
- **Population restrictions:** checked on every row.
  - One Major: OA-4.5 tells every person with knee OA to lose weight.
  - One Minor: OA-4.3 says 「需要減重的人」.
  - Knee/hip/hand scoping is otherwise correct: yoga is knee only; braces are knee only; heat/cold covers knee, hip and hand; weight loss is limited to overweight or obesity in OA-4.1, 4.2 and 4.4; TENS covers all OA via NICE.
- **EDITORIAL phrases (3): all neutral and harmless.** 「用藥請和醫師討論」, 「使用時注意溫度，避免燙傷或凍傷」 and 「醫師因其他原因開立的維生素D，請照醫囑使用」 (the last is protective). No change is needed.

  - OA-5.3: 「長期配戴」 may be read as all-day wear.
