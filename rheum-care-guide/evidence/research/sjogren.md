- **PMC full text**: read through PubMed MCP `get_full_text_article`, and checked for glued words / dropped formatted words (see Notes).

## E. Could not verify / open questions

1. **EULAR 2020 (S4) recommendation numbers and GoR.** Numbering differed between WebFetch extractions (ocular first-line labelled 3 or 4; fatigue 5 or 7; exercise text under 6, 8 or 9). The full 1–12 enumeration (consistent with "12 specific recommendations" in the abstract) gives 2 = oral, 3 = ocular, 5 = fatigue/pain, 6 = musculoskeletal pain. **Cite by statement text.** GoR for the dryness statements was not found in accessible text. The PDF returned 429; the Elsevier mirror and repository PDF returned 403.
2. **Foulks 2015 (S6), Zero 2016 (S7), Carsons 2017 (S8) full texts not accessible.** Publisher pages returned 403; the Ocul Surf page permission request timed out. Content relies on PubMed abstracts plus Sjögren's Foundation summary handouts (S22–S24, secondary).
3. **Moisture-chamber spectacles strength (Foulks/S22):** extractions disagreed (STRONG vs MODERATE STRONG; evidence "good" vs "moderate"). **Strength not reliably confirmed.** Use S11 (mechanism) and S14 ("promising") instead.
4. **TFOS DEWS III (S11):** only ~40–45% of the pre-proof was processed. The lifestyle section, prescribing algorithm and omega-3 conclusion were **not read**. Pre-proof wording may differ from the published version (AJO site 403). PubMed also lists AJO vol 288 e-page items titled "TFOS DEWS III." (PMIDs 41276204, 41276205, 41110677, 40997989); their relation to the 2025 reports was not examined.
5. **TFOS Lifestyle reports (S14–S16):** abstract only.
6. **Dental check-up interval:** no source read specifies how often to see the dentist. BSR says only "proactive dental care"; Zero (via S23) gives no recall interval. **No claim possible.**
7. **Skin dryness self-care (emollients, bathing):** no guideline-level recommendation found. BSR 2024 notes dry/itchy skin is common but gives no advice. The BSR 2017 portion read had none.
8. **Nasal dryness (saline sprays, nasal humidification):** no guideline-level recommendation found. Only humidification for chronic cough (C49).
9. **Acidic foods/drinks and dental erosion:** no explicit statement found. There is tension between EULAR's "sugar-free acidic candies" (C30) and the Brazilian guideline's preference for stimulants keeping "a less acid pH" and "keeping intraoral pH (basic)" (C43, C46). Do not write "avoid acidic foods/drinks" without a source.
10. **Caffeine:** only the Brazilian guideline (grade D, expert opinion) advises avoiding caffeinated drinks (C63); no other source addresses it.
11. **Smoking in SjD:** only Brazilian (D) and TFOS associations (C63, C64). BSR 2024 and EULAR 2020 do not address it.
12. **Drying medicines:** the explicit "avoid drying systemic medication" recommendation (C18, C51) comes from the **superseded** BSR 2017. BSR 2024 does not discuss it; whether it was deliberately dropped is unknown. Current support: Foulks/S22 (secondary), DEWS III exec summary, WWOM VI SR. **No source states "do not stop prescribed medicines without medical advice"**; if used on the site, label it as an editorial safety note.
13. **Vaccination (C70):** exact wording only partially obtained (fragments). The fragment "live attenuated vaccines should be avoided" lacked a confirmed population (likely immunosuppressed patients) and was not recorded as a claim.
14. **Brazilian 2021 gynaecology/obstetric recommendations** (PMID 34479630; DOI 10.1186/s42358-021-00208-1) not read (no PMC; Springer rate-limited). May contain vaginal lubricant/moisturiser advice.
15. **TSCRS (S21) treatment table** (exact step-1 lifestyle items) not read.
16. **Pulmonary guideline (S9)** individual recommendation strengths (e.g. humidification for chronic cough) are in tables not present in the extract.
17. **Diet:** BSR reports an observational association: "Adherence to a Mediterranean diet was associated with a lower likelihood of having SD." That is a risk association, not a management recommendation; not recorded as a claim.
18. **Vitamin D:** BSR recommends baseline vitamin D testing ("Vitamin D levels; (1, B) (SOA 95.6%)") and Brazil advises investigating deficiency. These are clinician tests, not lifestyle; not recorded as claims.
19. **Pregnancy planning:** BSR states "Many units offer pre-pregnancy counselling to discuss the risks." That is descriptive (UK practice), not a recommendation. The related recommendations are drug-based (HCQ, aspirin), so out of scope.
20. **Traditional Chinese medicine:** only a small Taiwan RCT (Tier 3, negative primary endpoint) was found; not usable per protocol. No guideline addresses TCM.
21. **Sjögren's Foundation updates:** the Foundation lists Phase-2 oral guidelines (mucosal management, secretagogues, caries management/restoration) as in development. The 2026 PNS guideline (Deboo A et al., Arthritis Care Res 2026;78(7):860-874; PMID 41327784; PMC13313102; DOI 10.1002/acr.70004) exists but concerns neuropathy, not lifestyle, and was not read beyond the abstract.
