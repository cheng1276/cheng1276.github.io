# Evidence file: Axial spondyloarthritis / ankylosing spondylitis (僵直性脊椎炎)

Compiled: 2026-10-06. Scope: non-pharmacological self-care / lifestyle and "seek care promptly" statements, per PROTOCOL.md. No patient-facing Chinese text is included.

**How sources were read (important for auditing)**
- PMC full text via `get_full_text_article`: S3, S4, S6, S10, S20 (and abstract/plain-language summary only for S13; PMC body text was empty).
- Publisher or institutional-repository pages via WebFetch (shell egress to these hosts was blocked by policy): S1 (ard.bmj.com page + Leiden University repository copy of the publisher version), S2 (ACR summary PDF), S5 (NICE web page + NICE PDF), S7 (University of Jyväskylä repository, published version, CC BY 4.0), S9 (ard.bmj.com page + University of Oviedo repository PDF). WebFetch passes text through a summarising model, so every quote from these sources was re-requested with a differently worded prompt; only wording that matched across at least two passes is used. Exceptions are flagged as "single pass".
- Abstract only (PubMed metadata): all other Tier-2/3 sources.
- Dropped-word pitfall: every PMC quote used was checked for glued words. Glued tokens seen in S10/S20/S6 (e.g. "treatmentand", ")and") are removed citation numbers, not dropped words; quotes were cut to avoid them or flagged. One truncated sentence in S10 (ends "common in.") is quoted only up to the intact part. In S4 the Arthritis & Rheumatology PMC version DROPPED the list-formatted recommendation statements (PICO 16-18, 52-55); they were recovered intact from the co-published Arthritis Care & Research PMC version (PMC5123844) and match the rationale text in both versions.
