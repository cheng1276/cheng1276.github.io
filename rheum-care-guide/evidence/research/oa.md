## Reading notes (please read before using any row)

- **Superseded and new guidelines (check this first).** The ACR has posted a **2026 update** of its OA recommendations (S3). So far it exists only as an official **summary PDF**, approved by the ACR Board on 8 September 2026 and posted on 14 September 2026. The full manuscript "will be submitted for publication in Arthritis & Rheumatology and Arthritis Care & Research". S3 is not peer reviewed and has no PubMed record. Where S3 and the 2019 ACR/AF guideline (S1/S2) differ, S3 is the current ACR position. The differences are:
  - tai chi, tibiofemoral knee braces and thumb-base (first CMC) orthoses changed from strong to conditional;
  - self-management programmes and canes changed from strong recommendations to ungraded good practice statements;
  - massage and chondroitin for hand OA changed direction (see E). Other updates found and used: **EULAR 2023** hip/knee non-pharmacological update (S7; supersedes EULAR 2013, S8), **EULAR 2025** physical activity update (S10; supersedes EULAR 2018, S11), and the **Cochrane exercise reviews** for knee (2024, S15) and hip (2026, S16). No newer OARSI (2019), NICE NG226 (2022; last minor change October 2023) or EULAR hand OA (2018) guideline was found.
- **Quotes extracted from PMC (access "full text (PMC)")**: the tool strips citation numbers, which leaves empty brackets such as "()". These brackets are reproduced exactly as extracted. It also **drops formatted words**. In the ACR 2019 guideline (both PMC versions, S1 and S2) it dropped "against" from every negative recommendation heading, leaving glued words: "recommendedin" and "recommendedover". Those headings are quoted exactly as extracted. The Notes column says how each direction was confirmed: through S4 (the AAFP summary of ACR 2019), through explicit sentences in the ACR text, or through S3 (the 2026 update marks every change of direction since 2019 with an asterisk, so a row without an asterisk has the same direction as in 2019).
- **Quotes from PubMed abstracts** are verbatim, except that non-breaking spaces in the source (for example "OR = 0.97" in S23) are written as ordinary spaces.
- **ACR 2019 quotes are taken from S2** (Arthritis Care & Research version, PMC11488261). S1 (Arthritis & Rheumatology version, PMC10518852) has identical wording, but its PMC text contains stray characters, for example "GFiADE", "knee g and/or hip", "comgbines" and "ROT evidence".
- **Quotes obtained through WebFetch (access "full text via WebFetch")**: S3, S4, S5 (tables only), S6, S9, S10 and S12 were read through a fetch tool that passes the page through a summarising model. That model often refuses quotes longer than about 125 characters, so long recommendations were requested in short consecutive segments, shown as "…" "…" in the quote column. Several key quotes were re-requested in a second pass and matched: S3 (all main rows), S6 (1.2.2, 1.3.3, 1.3.5, 1.3.8, 1.3.11, 1.4.6), S9 (Table 1 values for Recommendations 1, 2, 3 and 6), S10 (the Recommendation 5 text and the flare sentence) and S12 (the statements used). The remaining quotes were read once; they are listed in Section E. **Check every WebFetch-derived quote character by character against the original PDF before publication.**
- **Confidence**: High = explicit in the full text of a Tier-1 document read in this session. Medium = Tier 2, or a Tier-1 abstract only, or **any S3 row**. S3 rows are Medium because S3 is a pre-publication summary with no evidence text, it is not peer reviewed, and it was read only through WebFetch. Low = secondary source or indirect evidence.
- **Medication-adjacent** rows are patient-behaviour messages only. No prescription-drug dosing is recorded. Injections (hyaluronic acid, PRP, steroids) and GLP-1 receptor agonists are outside the lifestyle scope and are not recorded as claims (see E).
- Abbreviations: OA osteoarthritis; CMC carpometacarpal (thumb base); TF tibiofemoral; PF patellofemoral; GPS good practice statement; LoE level of evidence; GoR/SoR grade/strength of recommendation; LoA level of agreement (0–10); PA physical activity; SB sedentary behaviour; IA inflammatory arthritis; HCP health care professional; AHP allied health professional; SR systematic review; RCT randomised controlled trial.
