# Evidence file: cross-cutting lifestyle care for rheumatic and musculoskeletal diseases (shared section)

Scope: gout, chronic urticaria, RA, axSpA, SLE, Sjögren's (shared page). Topics: weekly physical activity (amount, type), reducing sedentary time, smoking cessation, healthy weight and diet, alcohol, bone health on glucocorticoids (calcium/vitamin D, weight-bearing exercise, falls), vaccination principles, UV index.
Compiled: 2026-10-06. Protocol: /home/claude/research/PROTOCOL.md.

Access method notes (apply to the whole file):
- "Full text (PMC)" = read with get_full_text_article. Known dropped-word pitfall checked: glued words were found in S4 and S10 and handled (see Notes for each claim).
- "Full text (WebFetch)" = publisher, repository or agency PDF/page read through WebFetch. WebFetch passes the page through a summarising model. I asked for verbatim text every time, and re-read key tables 2 or 3 times: S1 Table 1 (twice, identical), S6 recommendation (3 times), S7 recommendation (3 times), S11 threshold (twice). Single-read quotes are marked "single read".
- Important population caveat. S1 (EULAR lifestyle) covers OA, RA, axSpA, PsA, SLE, SSc and gout. S2 (EULAR PA 2025) covers inflammatory arthritis (RA, SpA) and hip/knee OA. Neither covers chronic urticaria or Sjögren's directly. WHO statements (S4, S5) are for the general population and selected chronic conditions. S1 overarching principle 3 bridges them to RMDs.

## E. Could not verify / open questions / conflicts

1. **CONFLICT: vaccination and disease activity.** EULAR 2019 (C64): "preferably ... during quiescent disease". ACR 2022 (C73): non-live vaccines "regardless of patients' disease activity" (conditional, very low evidence). Suggested wording for editors: shared decision with the rheumatologist; do not delay flu vaccination (C72).
2. **DIFFERENCE: zoster vaccination.** EULAR 2019: "may be considered in high-risk patients" (the abstract says "should be administered to AIIRD patients at risk"). ACR 2022: recombinant zoster vaccine strongly recommended for all over 18 on immunosuppression. EULAR 2019 predates the 2022 ACR guideline. The Taiwan guidance (S21), endorsed by the Taiwan College of Rheumatology, could settle this locally, but its text was not accessible.
3. **DIFFERENCE: live vaccines.** EULAR: "may be considered with caution". ACR: "deferring live attenuated vaccines is conditionally recommended" for those on immunosuppression. Compatible direction (caution or defer); different strength of wording.
4. **DROPPED WORDS (S10, PMC extraction).** Glued "medication,live", "RMD,methotrexate", "rituximab,non–live", "daily,non–live" and "RMD,immunosuppressive" all drop "deferring" or "holding". Only the live-vaccine statement was used: its direction was confirmed by the ACR-hosted PDF ("deferring") and the intact Discussion ("conditionally recommended against administering"). The others are drug-timing statements and were intentionally excluded.
5. **Vitamin D serum targets differ.** ACR 2017: "≥20 ng/ml". ACR 2022: "≥30 to 50 ng/mL". ECTS 2024: "50-125 nmol/L". These are clinician-level targets; suggest not printing serum targets on the patient site.
6. **Secondary-source error flagged.** S23 (2026 narrative review) states: "The ACR recommends that all patients taking GCs also take at least 1500 mg of calcium and 800 IU of vitamin D every day []." This contradicts the ACR 2017 and 2022 texts read directly (calcium 1,000–1,200 mg/day; vitamin D 600–800 IU/day or more). It also contradicts S23's own section 5.3 ("adequate calcium intake (1000–1200 mg/day) and vitamin D supplementation (800–1000 IU/day)"). Do NOT use S23 for amounts.
7. **ACR 2022 certainty label wording.** Two reads gave "Low or very low" and "Low to very low". Both mean low or very low certainty. Exact punctuation not confirmed.
8. **Version checks.** The ACR 2022 GIOP manuscript is the ACR-hosted version; it was not compared with the typeset Wiley version (blocked). The S1 PDF is the online-first version ("Volume 0, pages 1–9"); it was not compared with the print version 2023;82:48–56.
9. **Alcohol amounts.** EULAR (S1) does not define "low" or "moderate" (searched; not found). The informing SR (S14) used heterogeneous definitions, e.g. "Moderate intake(≤20 g/day for women,≤30 g/day for men)" in one RA study and "Moderate consumption(>2 drinks/day for men and one drink/day for women)" in a gout study (S14 Tables 6–7). The bone-health alcohol limit (ACR: ≤2 servings/day) is a ceiling for GC users, not a "safe" endorsement, and may sit uneasily with the RA and gout flare advice (C52, C53). Editors should present it carefully.
10. **UV index wording.** (a) WHO 2002 guide: "below a UVI of 3 is limited". WHO Q&A 2022: "below a UVI of 2 is limited". Both agree that protection is needed at 3 and above, and both use bands 0/1–2, 3–7 and 8+. (b) "Low" is printed as "< 2" in the 2002 Table 1, "1-2" in its Figure 2, and "0 to 2" (WHO Q&A) or "0-2" (CWA) elsewhere. Suggest 0–2 following the CWA.
11. **EULAR 2025 PA (S2) sentence with a missing word.** "Studies in IA and OA reported no detrimental of exercise on disease activity." Read twice; the tool says it is as printed. Probably "no detrimental [effect]", but this was not independently confirmed, so it is not used. Exercise safety rests on S1 (C11).
12. **EULAR 2019 vaccination (S9).** LoE, SoR and LoA for each principle and recommendation are in Tables 1–2, which were not accessible. Recorded as "strength not found in accessible text".
13. **EULAR 2024 SLE/SSc (S17).** SoR not found; recommendation wording read once via WebFetch.
14. **WHO chronic-condition recommendations are indirect for RMDs.** They were developed from evidence in cancer survivors, hypertension, type 2 diabetes and HIV (S4). Application to RMDs is supported by S1 OP 3 and S2 OAP 3.
15. **Pages not read.** Europe PMC REST API returned HTTP 429 (WebFetch said not to retry). ScienceDirect, e-jmii.com and jfma-online.com are blocked by robots.txt. ard.eular.org, Wiley, WHO IRIS and the UMC Utrecht PDF returned 403. The shell (curl) was blocked by proxy policy for iris.who.int, digibuo.uniovi.es, jyx.jyu.fi and assets.contentstack.io.
16. **Not covered by these guidelines.** COVID-19 vaccination is excluded from ACR 2022 (S10); the EULAR SARS-CoV-2 November 2021 update (PMID 35197264) was not reviewed. Chronic urticaria and Sjögren's are not explicitly included in S1 or S2; extrapolation to them is by general WHO principles only. A Brazilian Society of Rheumatology 2025 vaccination recommendation exists (PMID 41578353) and was not used (not a hierarchy-priority body for this site).
17. **WebFetch transcription risk.** All WebFetch quotes passed through a summarising model. Key tables were re-read and matched (S1 Table 1 twice, identical; S6 three times; S7 three times). Single-read quotes are marked in the Notes; minor wording differences cannot be fully excluded for those.
