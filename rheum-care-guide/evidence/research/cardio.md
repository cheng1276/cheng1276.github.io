# Evidence file: cardiovascular risk in rheumatic and musculoskeletal diseases (心血管風險) — slug "cardio"

Scope: which RMDs carry higher cardiovascular (CV) risk and how much; regular CV risk assessment (who, how often); traditional risk factors managed as in the general population, with disease-specific targets; disease-activity control; glucocorticoids and NSAIDs (generic classes) in relation to CV risk; drug-specific items as background only; seek-care statements; myths. For the common page's planned card on CV risk.

## Reading notes

- **Access codes.** *FT-PMC* = full text returned by the PubMed `get_full_text_article` tool. *FT-WF* = full text read with WebFetch (publisher page or repository PDF). WebFetch passes pages through a summarising model and returns quotes of ≤125 characters, so long sentences came back as consecutive fragments; they are re-joined here with single spaces. "2 reads" = two independent fetches returned identical wording (for long sentences the second read sometimes confirmed only the opening ≤125 characters; this is stated). "Single read" = seen once; spot-check before publication. *Abs* = PubMed abstract only. *Sec* = secondary source.
- **Citation markers.** In FT-WF quotes, superscript reference numbers are shown as "[ref]". In FT-PMC quotes, stripped citations appear as "[]", "[,]" or "()" and are kept verbatim; they are not missing words.
- **Quote verification.** All FT-PMC quotes (S5, S6, S7, S10, S14) and the Tier-2 abstract quotes (S18–S24) were checked by exact substring match against the saved tool outputs; all matched. The only differences were non-breaking/thin spaces in some abstracts (e.g. "n = 10", "RR = 2.37"), normalised here to ordinary spaces.
- **Extraction-pitfall check (dropped formatted words / glued words).** Every FT-PMC quote used here was checked. Glued words found: S5 "dyslipidemiaand" and "risk.A" (stripped citation markers; the meaning of the "dyslipidemiaand" sentence was confirmed from the S5 abstract, an independent copy); S14 "riskenhancing" (hyphen lost; no direction at stake) and "offats" (dropped italic word, probably "trans"; that sentence was not used); S6 "flareplacebo" (likely dropped "versus"; not used). No for/against direction in this file rests on an unconfirmed PMC sentence. The key "not recommended" direction (S1 rec 7, aspirin) comes from publisher HTML (2 reads), not from PMC.
- **Dosing.** Prescription-drug doses are not recorded. Where a verbatim quote contained a dose, it is replaced by "[dose omitted]" or "[…]". Glucocorticoid mg/day figures that describe *exposure levels in observational studies* (S1, S3) are kept because the addendum asks for dose/duration relations "exactly as stated"; they are not dosing advice and are flagged "not for site text as a number".
- **Drug-specific items** (aspirin, urate-lowering therapy, hydroxychloroquine, immunosuppressants, diuretics, beta-blockers, ACE inhibitors/ARBs, statins, named NSAIDs) are marked **"drug-specific — not for site text"**. Generic principles are recorded as separate claims. "Glucocorticoids" and "NSAIDs" are generic classes and are allowed by the addendum.
- **Confidence.** High = explicit in Tier-1 full text read this session (PMC, or WebFetch with consistent reads). Medium = Tier-2; Tier-1 abstract only; Tier-1 single WebFetch read; Tier-3 landmark numbers read in an abstract or full text. Low = secondary source (incl. the official EULAR lay summary S2), derived wording, or indirect population. Where a clinician-directed statement is turned into a patient message, both levels are given.
- **Population caveats.** S1 covers gout, vasculitis, SSc, myositis, MCTD, Sjögren's, SLE and APS; S3 covers RA, AS and PsA. Together they cover all site diseases except chronic urticaria and OA (no CV guidance sought for those). S4 covers OA, RA, axSpA, PsA, SLE, SSc and gout. S12–S15 are general-population cardiology guidance (indirect).
- **Site context respected.** The common page already covers smoking, healthy diet, weight, alcohol and physical-activity amounts. Lifestyle claims here are limited to statements that link those behaviours to CV risk in RMDs (useful as bridges), not to amounts or food lists.
- **Access limits this session.** The shell could not download PDFs (proxy policy 403 for research-portal.uea.ac.uk; not retried). WebFetch failed on the S1 accepted-manuscript PDFs (UEA, Aberdeen Pure) and on the ESC Essential Messages PDF; Europe PMC links were refused (not in provenance set). Tables 1 of S1 and S3 (verbatim overarching principles, LoE/SoR/LoA) were not rendered on the publisher pages.
- Existing evidence files (ra.md, sle.md, common.md, gout.md) were used only as leads; every quote below was re-read in this session.
