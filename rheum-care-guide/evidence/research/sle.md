# Evidence file: Systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE, 紅斑性狼瘡), lifestyle and self-care, including cutaneous lupus aspects relevant to SLE

Built: 2026-10-06 (session date). Built under /home/claude/research/PROTOCOL.md. This is an evidence file only. It contains no patient-facing Chinese text.

How to read this file (access caveats that apply throughout):
- "PMC full text" = text returned by the PubMed MCP `get_full_text_article` tool. In these texts, reference citations are stripped and sometimes appear as `[]` or `[,]`. These are kept verbatim inside quotes and are not missing words.
- "WebFetch" = publisher or repository page read through the WebFetch tool. That tool passes pages through a summarising model and limits each quote to about 125 characters. Long quotes were therefore requested in consecutive verbatim segments and joined here. Where a WebFetch quote was retrieved only once, the Notes say so. Where it was retrieved two or more times with identical wording, the Notes say "consistent across N fetches". A human spot-check of WebFetch-derived table values (Parodis 2024 Table 1, EULAR 2023 Table 1) against the PDF is recommended before publication.
- Glued-word check: every quote taken from PMC full text was checked for glued words or odd grammar, the extraction pitfall named in the protocol. Quotes affected by dropped words were either not used or used only after independent confirmation, and the Notes say which.
- Confidence: High = explicit in Tier-1 full text read in this session. Medium = Tier 2, Tier-1 abstract only, Tier-1 text read through a single WebFetch retrieval, or Tier-1 text whose population is indirect (e.g., all RMDs). Low = secondary, translated, or indirect.

## E. Could not verify / open questions

Access limitations and verification caveats
1. Parodis 2024 (S04) Table 1 values (LoE, SoR, LoA) came through WebFetch, an AI-mediated reading of the version-of-record PDF. Wording and values were consistent across repeated queries. However, one query mis-numbered the SLE items, and the abstract says "SoR ranged from A to D" while every SoR I extracted was B, C or D. Table 1 should be spot-checked by a human. The SoR "B/C" (smoking) and "C/D" (cold) entries were printed jointly for SLE/SSc. The SLE-specific letter was not confirmed on the PDF.
2. EULAR 2023 (S03) quotes came through WebFetch in ≤125-character segments. They were consistent across fetches. The LoE/SoR for the skin recommendation and the full discussion of bone health and vaccination were not retrieved.
3. Andreoli 2017 (S06): the 12 Table 1 recommendation statements, with their LoE/GoR, are not in the PMC text or the publisher HTML. One WebFetch call returned plausible-looking "statements" with LoE/GoR. A follow-up check confirmed Table 1 is not present on that page, so that output was fabricated by the summariser and was discarded. Only body-text sentences were used. Glued-word passages were not used: "There is no concrete evidence that the diseasedecreases fertility" and "In women with(), contraception with combined hormones ... should be discouraged".
4. European S2k CLE guideline (S21, Kuhn 2017) full text was inaccessible. Its statements on SPF, UVA, amount, timing, reapplication, clothing and artificial light could not be verified.
5. German S2k CLE guideline: the English JDDG version (Worm 2021, Parts 1–2) was inaccessible. The German AWMF PDF was used, with my translations (C36–C41, M10). Its validity was extended only to 24.3.2025, and no newer version was found, so current status is uncertain. The smoking section (7.2, "Rauchen") could not be read in full. No smoking recommendation box was retrieved.
6. The BAD 2021 CLE guideline (S29) was not accessed (no PubMed abstract; DOI page rate-limited). It likely contains UK dermatology photoprotection recommendations and may also be the source behind BSR's adoption of SPF at least 30 and reapplication at least every two hours (C28–C29). BSR cites "the BAD", but the exact BAD document is not identified in the extracted text.
7. ACR 2025 (S07): only the pre-publication summary was read. The final published good-practice-statement wording is unverified.
8. ACR 2020 reproductive health (S08), EULAR CV (S16), EULAR vaccination 2019 (S17), Parisis 2019 (S18), Chasset 2015 (S19), Kuhn 2011 (S20), Fairley 2019 (S30), French protocol (S25), Cochrane (S10), He 2026 (S11), Fang 2026 (S12) and Santos 2023 (S14) were all abstract only.
9. EULAR lifestyle (S15) and EULAR fatigue (S13): statement wording was retrieved in segments through WebFetch, but LoE/SoR/LoA tables were not accessible ("strength not found in accessible text").

Not found in any accessible source (do not write these claims without a new source)
10. Indoor artificial light (fluorescent, halogen or LED lamps, screens) and lupus. No guideline statement was found. The German guideline (as read) mentions only artificial UV in tanning studios.
11. A numeric UV-index threshold. S04 says only "days with high UV index".
12. Sunscreen reapplication after swimming, sweating or towelling. Water-resistant products. Lip and eye protection.
13. Taiwan or Asia UVA labelling conventions. BSR's UVA advice uses UK/EU labels (UVA logo, 1–5 stars). A local source is needed before naming any label system.
14. Peak-UV clock times for Taiwan. The only clock times found (11:00–15:00) are from the German guideline. S09 says peak hours vary by location.
15. Infection-prevention behaviours beyond vaccination, such as hand hygiene, avoiding sick contacts, food safety or pet/garden precautions. No SLE guideline statement was found. Household-member vaccination (C136) is the only household measure found.
16. SLE-specific alcohol guidance. Only all-RMD EULAR guidance (C155) was found.
17. Sleep-hygiene advice specific to SLE. No evidence was found (M08).
18. Work participation in SLE. EULAR 2021 (S15) covers work for RMDs generally. It was not extracted here.

Conflicts flagged (record both sides on the website or choose conservatively)
19. Exercise and fatigue. EULAR recommends aerobic exercise to reduce fatigue (LoE 1–3, SoR B; C71), and He 2026 rates fatigue benefit as moderate certainty (C85). Cochrane (C84) and the 2026 umbrella review (C86) rate the evidence as low certainty with an uncertain or slight benefit. Safe wording: exercise is recommended and may modestly reduce fatigue.
20. Psychoeducation and fatigue. EULAR recommends it for fatigue across inflammatory RMDs (C99), but the SLE subgroup showed no significant effect (M12).
21. Vitamin D. BSR recommends a universal daily supplement (1B; C120). EULAR non-pharmacological text advises assessing the need when indicated (C22).
22. Sun-exposure wording. EULAR says "avoid sun exposure" (C24) and "avoid direct sun exposure" (C22). BSR says "reduced sun exposure" as a safe-sun strategy (C26). This is a difference of emphasis, not of direction.
23. Green tea extract (M04) and complementary-medicine associations (M13) versus immunostimulatory-herb concerns (M01).
24. Cochrane search date. BSR's text cites the Cochrane exercise review "with a search date up until 30 March 2021", but the Cochrane abstract states "The latest search date was 30 March 2022". The Cochrane primary source is taken as correct.
