# 僵直性脊椎炎（axspa）

簡介：這頁依據國際與台灣指引，整理僵直性脊椎炎的生活照護重點：規律運動、戒菸、保護脊椎、留意眼睛警訊，以及何時該就醫。

| AS-1.1 | 規律運動 | 盡早養成規律運動的習慣。運動是治療的基石，在用藥之外也對病情有幫助。 | ASAS-EULAR 2022・建議強度 B | **C01 / S1** (High; LoE/GoR "2b /B (education, exercise)", "5/D (stop smoking)", "1a /A (physiotherapy)"; LoA 9.8 (0.5), 100% ≥8): “Patients should be educated about axSpA and encouraged to exercise on a regular basis…”<br>**C09 / S1** (High; Rec 4 exercise component 2b/B): “Exercise is a cornerstone in the management of axSpA, with demonstrated benefits on disease outcomes independent of pharmacological treatment.”<br>**C20 / S10** (High; LoE IIa; GoR B; LoA 92.9%): “Patients with axSpA should be encouraged to stop smoking and start an individualized regular exercise program as soon as possible.…” |
| AS-1.2 | 規律運動 | 請物理治療師設計個人化運動計畫，包括伸展、肌力、姿勢、深呼吸、脊椎伸展、頸胸腰椎活動度與有氧運動。 | NICE 2017・建議 | **C21 / S5** (High; No grade; wording "Refer"): “Refer people with axial spondyloarthritis to a specialist physiotherapist to start an individualised, structured exercise programme, which should include: stretching, strengthening and postural exercises; deep breathing; spinal extension; range of motion exercises for the lumbar, thoracic and cervical sections of the spine; aerobic exercise.” |
| AS-1.3 | 規律運動 | 先由物理治療師教會動作，之後也建議在家自己做背部運動。 | ACR 2015・建議 | **C44 / S4** (High; Conditional; moderate-quality; 91%): “In adults with active or stable AS, we conditionally recommend advising unsupervised back exercises (PICO 20; moderate-quality evidence; vote 91% agreement). / The recommendation is conditional in that unsupervised back exercises should not substitute for initial instruction in back exercises by a physical therapist.” |
| AS-1.4 | 規律運動 | 有人指導時較能持之以恆；如果很難自己持續運動，可和醫師討論物理治療。 | ASAS-EULAR 2022・建議強度 A | **C42 / S1** (High; 1a/A): “On an individual basis, physiotherapy should be considered, especially if a patient does not exercise on their own.”<br>**C41 / S1** (High; Physiotherapy 1a/A): “While exercise in general should always be part of the disease management, evidence from qualitative studies shows that adherence is higher if supervised.” |
| AS-1.5 | 規律運動 | 接受姿勢衛教、做姿勢運動，避免長時間彎腰駝背。 | TRA 2020・指引說明 | **C62 / S10** (High; Ungraded narrative): “Postural education and exercise are important to help patients avoid positions that lead to prolonged stooping.” |
| AS-1.T | 規律運動（小提醒） | 發作或急性發炎時，避免高強度的激烈運動；可依發作計畫做伸展等自我照護。 | TRA 2020・指引說明 | **C61 / S10** (High; Ungraded narrative): “It is also important to avoid any intense strenuous exercise when there is a flare‐up or acute inflammation.”<br>**C60 / S5** (High; Wording "Advise" / "Consider" / "provide"): “When discussing any flare management plan, provide information on: … self-care (for example, exercises, stretching and joint protection)…” |
| AS-2.1 | 戒菸 | 如果有抽菸，請戒菸。抽菸對症狀、身體功能、疾病活動度、病情進展和其他疾病都有不良影響。 | EULAR 2021・建議強度 B | **C67 / S9** (High; 2a / B / 9.9 (0.2) (100)): “People with RMDs should be encouraged to stop smoking and be informed that smoking is detrimental to symptoms, function, disease activity, disease progression and occurrence of comorbidities in all RMDs”<br>**C64 / S1** (High; 5/D (stop smoking)): “…encouraged to exercise on a regular basis and stop smoking…” |
| AS-2.2 | 戒菸 | 研究發現，抽菸是脊椎發炎和病情進展的危險因子，X光上的脊椎變化也進展較快。 | ASAS-EULAR 2022・指引說明 | **C65 / S1** (High; - (Discussion of Recommendation 4)): “Smoking has been shown as a risk factor for spinal inflammation and disease progression in axSpA.”<br>**C73 / S6** (High; Narrative): “observational studies have shown that certain groups (e.g. males, smokers … ) have greater risk and rate of radiographic progression.” |
| AS-2.3 | 戒菸 | 確診後就開始戒菸，戒菸計畫可以提供協助。 | TRA 2020・指引說明 | **C72 / S10** (High; Rec 4 LoE IIa; GoR B (accompanying statement, ungraded)): “Therefore, all axSpA patients should be actively encouraged to stop smoking upon diagnosis, and smoking cessation programs may facilitate this.” |
| AS-3.1 | 保護脊椎 | 避免任何脊椎扳動手法、強力按摩（推拿）、頸椎牽引和容易碰撞的接觸性運動，因為脊椎常較脆弱，輕微外力就可能骨折。 | TRA 2020・指引說明 | **C86 / S10** (High; Ungraded narrative): “AS patients often have fragile and osteoporotic spines that can develop spinal fractures or dislocations from only mild trauma, and therefore contact sports, cervical traction, and spinal manipulation should be avoided.”<br>**C85 / S10** (High; Ungraded narrative): “However, any type of spinal manipulation should be avoided in axSpA patients due to the risk of osteoporosis and spinal fracture, and rheumatologists should expressly warn patients of the risk involved, especially with the strong massage and joint manipulation techniques common in [final word lost in extraction]” |
| AS-3.2 | 保護脊椎 | 脊椎已融合（黏在一起）或有嚴重脊椎骨質疏鬆的人，更不要接受整脊等脊椎扳動手法。 | ACR 2015・強烈不建議 | **C84 / S4** (High; Strong AGAINST; very low quality; 100%): “In adults with active or stable AS and spinal fusion or advanced spinal osteoporosis, we strongly recommend against treatment with spinal manipulation (PICO 21; very low-quality evidence; vote 100% agreement). / Several case reports of spine fractures, spinal cord injury, and paraplegia following chiropractic spinal manipulation…” |
| AS-3.3 | 保護脊椎 | 跌倒或受傷後，請找醫療人員檢查，尤其是疼痛加劇時，並告知醫師您有僵直性脊椎炎。脊椎已融合的人，輕微外傷也較容易骨折；懷疑骨折時，可能需要磁振造影或電腦斷層檢查。 | NICE 2017・建議 | **C92 / S5** (High; Wording "Advise"): “Advise people with axial spondyloarthritis that they may be prone to fractures, and should consult a healthcare professional following falls or physical trauma, particularly in the event of increased musculoskeletal pain.”<br>**C94 / S1** (High; 5/D (Discussion of Recommendation 15)): “…particularly in patients with ankylosis of the spine, as a fracture is then more likely to occur even with a minor trauma…”<br>**C94 / S1** (High; 5/D (Discussion of Recommendation 15)): “When suspected, appropriate imaging such as MRI and/or CT scanning should be performed, and an experienced spinal surgeon may need to be consulted”<br>**EDITORIAL / ** (EDITORIAL; Editorial phrase 「並告知醫師您有僵直性脊椎炎」): “” |
| AS-3.4 | 保護脊椎 | 有骨質疏鬆、脊椎大範圍融合或平衡不穩的人，可請醫療團隊評估跌倒風險並提供建議。 | ACR 2015・建議 | **C91 / S4** (High; Conditional; very low quality; 100%): “In adults with AS, we conditionally recommend fall evaluation and counseling (PICO 51; very low-quality evidence; vote 100% agreement). / Because falls can lead to spinal fractures and devastating neurologic consequences in some patients, the panel recommended fall evaluation and counseling for patients with osteoporosis, extensive spinal fusion, postural instability, or concomitant neurologic or musculoskeletal diseases that affect balance.” |
| AS-3.T | 保護脊椎（小提醒） | 台灣常以機車、腳踏車代步，宜及早和醫師討論骨質疏鬆與脊椎骨折的風險。 | TRA 2020・指引說明 | **C98 / S10** (High; Ungraded narrative): “Considering that bicycles and motorcycles are often used for transportation in Taiwan, the risk of osteoporosis and spinal fracture needs to be addressed as early as possible in axSpA patients, to prevent complications or even permanent disability arising as the result of an accident or fall while riding a bicycle or motorcycle.” |
| AS-4.1 | 眼睛警訊 | 眼睛痛、眼睛紅、怕光或視力模糊，可能是急性前葡萄膜炎（虹彩炎），請當天就看眼科。 | NICE 2017・建議 | **C102 / S5** (High; Wording "Refer"; "immediate (same-day)"): “Refer people for an immediate (same‑day) ophthalmological assessment if they have symptoms of acute anterior uveitis (for example, eye pain, eye redness, sensitivity to light or blurred vision).”<br>**C108 / S10** (High; Statement accompanying Rec 5 (Rec 5: LoE IV; GoR D; LoA 92.9%)): “…should be managed as an emergency to avoid complications… / …axSpA patients with anterior uveitis should be referred to an ophthalmologist as soon as possible…” |
| AS-4.2 | 眼睛警訊 | 急性前葡萄膜炎是骨骼肌肉以外最常見的表現，多達四分之一的病人可能發生。 | BSR 2025・指引說明 | **C104 / S6** (High; Narrative): “Acute anterior uveitis is the most common EMM that can occur in up to a quarter of people with axSpA…” |
| AS-4.3 | 眼睛警訊 | 疾病發作期間也可能出現葡萄膜炎，要留意眼睛症狀。 | NICE 2017・建議 | **C103 / S5** (High; Wording "Be aware"): “Be aware that uveitis can occur during flare episodes.…” |
| AS-4.4 | 眼睛警訊 | 急性虹彩炎應由眼科醫師治療，以減輕發作的嚴重度、縮短時間或減少併發症。 | ACR 2015・強烈建議 | **C107 / S4** (High; S4 Strong, very low): “In adults with AS and acute iritis, we strongly recommend treatment by an ophthalmologist to decrease the severity, duration, or complications of episodes (PICO 27; very low-quality evidence; vote 100% agreement).” |
| AS-5.1 | 認識疾病與支持 | 多了解這個病，是自我照護的起點，也能和醫療團隊一起做治療決定。 | ASAS-EULAR 2022・建議強度 B | **C01 / S1** (High; LoE/GoR "2b /B (education, exercise)", "5/D (stop smoking)", "1a /A (physiotherapy)"; LoA 9.8 (0.5), 100% ≥8): “Patients should be educated about axSpA…”<br>**C02 / S1** (High; Part of Rec 4 (education 2b/B)): “All patients should receive education about the disease, as the starting point of self-management, to empower and involve them as active partners in their care.”<br>**C08 / S10** (High; OP4 LoA 100%; statement ungraded): “To achieve shared decision‐making, patients need to be provided with adequate education about their disease…” |
| AS-5.2 | 認識疾病與支持 | 可以和醫療團隊一起擬定發作計畫：發作時聯絡誰、怎麼自我照護（如運動、伸展），以及如何處理疼痛和疲倦。 | NICE 2017・建議 | **C60 / S5** (High; Wording "Advise" / "Consider" / "provide"): “Consider developing a flare management plan that is tailored to the person's individual needs, preferences and circumstances. / When discussing any flare management plan, provide information on: access to care during flares (including details of a named person to contact [for example, a specialist rheumatology nurse]); self-care (for example, exercises, stretching and joint protection); pain and fatigue management; potential changes to medicines; managing the impact on daily life and ability to work.” |
| AS-5.3 | 認識疾病與支持 | 可以向醫療團隊詢問病友團體、網路論壇和公益團體等資源。 | NICE 2017・建議 | **C06 / S5** (High; No grade printed; wording "Provide"): “Provide explanations and information about spondyloarthritis, for example: … self-help options; … local support groups, online forums and national charities, and how to get in touch with them.” |
| AS-M1 | 常見迷思 | 【常見說法】運動有風險，多休息才能保護脊椎。　【說明】運動是安全的，什麼時候開始都不嫌晚；請選擇適合自己的運動，避免容易碰撞的接觸性運動，發作時也避免激烈運動。 | EULAR 2021・建議強度 A | **C11 / S9** (High; 1a / A / 9.9 (0.3) (100)): “People with RMDs should be advised that exercise is safe and that it is never too late to start exercising”<br>**C86 / S10** (High; Ungraded narrative): “AS patients often have fragile and osteoporotic spines that can develop spinal fractures or dislocations from only mild trauma, and therefore contact sports, cervical traction, and spinal manipulation should be avoided.”<br>**C61 / S10** (High; Ungraded narrative): “It is also important to avoid any intense strenuous exercise when there is a flare‐up or acute inflammation.” |
| AS-M2 | 常見迷思 | 【常見說法】按摩、熱療這類被動治療就夠了，不必運動。　【說明】按摩、熱療、超音波等被動治療可以輔助，但不能取代主動運動；按摩宜溫和，避免強力推拿或扳動脊椎。 | ACR 2015・建議 | **C52 / S4** (High; Conditional; very low quality; 82%): “We conditionally recommend active physical therapy interventions (supervised exercise) over passive physical therapy interventions (massage, ultrasound, heat) (PICO 17; very low-quality evidence; vote 82% agreement). / Passive interventions could supplement, but not substitute for, active physical therapy interventions.”<br>**C85 / S10** (High; Ungraded narrative): “However, any type of spinal manipulation should be avoided in axSpA patients due to the risk of osteoporosis and spinal fracture, and rheumatologists should expressly warn patients of the risk involved, especially with the strong massage and joint manipulation techniques common in [final word lost in extraction]” |
| AS-M3 | 常見迷思 | 【常見說法】某一種運動最有效，只要做那一種就好。　【說明】目前研究還無法斷定哪一種運動最好，可請物理治療師依個人情況設計運動計畫。 | ASAS-EULAR 2022・指引說明 | **C30 / S1** (High; - (Discussion of Recommendation 4)): “Of note, the heterogeneity and methodological limitations across studies on exercise and physiotherapy hamper a definitive conclusion regarding which exercises are best to perform.”<br>**C21 / S5** (High; No grade; wording "Refer"): “Refer people with axial spondyloarthritis to a specialist physiotherapist to start an individualised, structured exercise programme…” |
