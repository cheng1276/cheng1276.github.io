# 治療期間防感染（infection）

簡介：使用類固醇、免疫抑制劑、生物製劑等藥物治療風濕病時，某些感染可能更容易發生或更嚴重。這頁整理治療前篩檢、治療中追蹤和生病時的應對。

## 審閱備註（撰寫者）
- HEPATITIS B, STOPPING ANTIVIRALS: no source read says 'do not stop antiviral prophylaxis on your own' (evidence file Section E1). After the fact-check, IF-3.5 and IF-M4 carry 「請不要自行停用」 as EDITORIAL. Everything else is sourced: antivirals continue for a while after immunosuppressive therapy ends (AASLD 2018: during IST and for at least 6 months after, at least 12 months after anti-CD20 therapy; EULAR 2022: 'reasonable' for at least 6–12 months, possibly longer with rituximab, but the task force explicitly made no recommendation; APASL 2021: consider stopping 6 months after IST for specified groups). Stopping happens 'under the guidance of a hepatologist' (APASL), and monitoring continues for up to 12 months after antivirals stop (AASLD). Because no source supports stopping antivirals when immunosuppression stops, 「通常」 was removed from IF-3.5, and IF-M4 now opens 「不應該跟著停」 (AASLD 'should continue'). No durations are printed, because they are clinician-level and differ between societies.
- STEROIDS (IF-4.2, IF-4.3, IF-S2) rest on an endocrinology guideline (ESE/ES 2024), which covers anyone on systemic glucocorticoids, not on a rheumatology guideline. IF-4.2: the adrenal-crisis statement covers 'oral supraphysiologic doses', rendered 「口服劑量高於身體自然分泌量時」; 「不要漏吃或自行突然停用類固醇」 is an inference plus the editorial no-self-stopping phrase (the verifier judged it safe). IF-4.3 (revised after the fact-check): it now names 「長期使用或最近才停用類固醇的人」 (R 3.1 'current or recent' use; R 2.1: under 3–4 weeks is 'low concern for HPA axis suppression'), says why (「以免腎上腺功能不足」, from the adrenal-crisis definition, C65), and keeps 「可能需要」 because Table 8 says patients already on higher doses 'typically' need no increase. 「（不是治療感染）」 and 「感染也要就醫」 are EDITORIAL clarifiers. R 3.1's condition 'did not undergo biochemical testing to rule out glucocorticoid-induced adrenal insufficiency' is still not spelled out. No stress-dose amounts or thresholds are printed. Please confirm you want patients told that a temporary steroid increase may be needed. IF-S2 (revised) is now directly sourced: R 3.2 (good clinical practice) says to consider adrenal crisis in current or recent users with vomiting or diarrhoea 'irrespective of the glucocorticoid type, mode of administration, and dose', and Table 8 calls for parenteral steroids when patients cannot take tablets. The ESE steroid emergency card is not mentioned, because no Taiwanese equivalent was checked.
- CONFLICTS ON SCREENING SCOPE, written as common ground (BRIEF rule 5). HIV: EULAR 2022 recommends it before bDMARDs and says consider it before others (GoR D); BSR 2025 says all adults starting a csDMARD except HCQ/mepacrine (1B); JAKi 2024 says high-risk populations. IF-1.4 therefore says only 「醫師也可能安排」. HBV: BSR 2025 exempts HCQ/mepacrine and EULAR Rec 4 states no exemption, so IF-3.1 says 「開始免疫抑制治療前」 rather than 'any antirheumatic drug', and keeps EULAR's 'glucocorticoids (according to dose and duration)'. TB before csDMARDs, immunosuppressants or glucocorticoids applies only to people at increased risk (EULAR 'considered'; BSR 2025 1B), hence 「也可能需要」 in IF-2.2. APLAR 2024 Statement 12 (screen for TB, HBV and HCV before any DMARD; strong, GRADE A) was not used because it is an RA recommendation and this page serves all rheumatic diseases. It could be added scoped to RA.
- TAIWAN CDC CHAPTER (S14): the book title and edition are not printed (probably a post-2022 edition). In this drafting session, two WebFetch reads confirmed that §10.3.5 is headed 「接受抗腫瘤壞死因子治療的病人」. That section holds the immune-rheumatic TB-risk sentence (IF-2.1, IF-M3), the TNF timing rule and the 都治 sentence. The 都治 sentence reads 「建議所有接受 LTBI 治療者參加都治計畫」, so IF-2.5 says 「接受潛伏結核治療時」, although strictly it sits in the anti-TNF section. The same sentence opens by saying this group is not a CDC-subsidised group (IGRA and co-payment). That seems at odds with §10.2.1, which lists anti-TNF users as a policy target group; the conflict is unresolved, and no cost or subsidy statement is printed. The rule of at least 4 weeks of LTBI treatment before a TNF blocker is not printed either (clinician-level timing); IF-2.4 says only 「通常要先開始潛伏結核治療，再用生物製劑」. Protection: S14 says 「約 9 成」 and S15 says 「9成以上」; the page uses 「約 9 成」. Both URLs were opened with the www. prefix on 2026-10-10. The independent fact-check confirmed these locations (§10.3.5, pp. 132–133; §10.2.5, p. 129) and the subsidy inconsistency; you may want to confirm current 都治 access for this group.
- RESOLVED HBV, MONITORING VERSUS PROPHYLAXIS: monitoring intervals differ (EULAR 'eg, every 3–6 months'; AGA 2025 1–3 months, abstract only; APASL 12 weeks in some groups; TRA 2012 via S12 monthly for 3 months, then every 3 months; ACR 'frequent'), so IF-3.4 says 「定期」. Who gets prophylaxis also differs. ACR conditionally prefers monitoring alone for non-rituximab b/tsDMARDs. TRA 2012 'recommended' prophylaxis but described treating only after HBV DNA or HBsAg turns positive, because of cost. APASL decides by drug risk group. All agree that rituximab is high risk. IF-3.4's 「部分用藥則可能需要抗病毒藥」 stands in for rituximab/anti-CD20, which BRIEF rule 7 does not let me name.
