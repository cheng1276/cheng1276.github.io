## 審閱備註（撰寫者）
- CONFLICT (BRIEF rule 5): DRUG TREATMENT OF HIGH URIC ACID WITHOUT GOUT. ACR 2020 conditionally recommends AGAINST starting urate-lowering therapy (certainty of evidence high), and says this also applies to people with CKD, CVD, kidney stones, hypertension or crystals on imaging (C48-C50). The Japanese guideline, 3rd ed. (read only through a secondary source, S40, Low) suggests considering drugs at ≥9 mg/dL, or ≥8 mg/dL with complications, after lifestyle change, and conditionally recommends them for hyperuricaemia with kidney injury (C58, C59). The Chinese 2024 guideline (endocrinology society) says drugs should be started at ≥9 mg/dL or with cardiovascular, metabolic or kidney comorbidities (C61). Korea 2023 made no recommendation for CKD 3-4 (C63). EULAR 2018/2016 and HKSR 2023 make no recommendation. APLAR 2021 has 2 statements that neither the evidence agent nor the fact-checker could read (C66). The Taiwan 2018 consensus was read in abstract only. The abstract states no position on drug treatment for hyperuricaemia without gout, and its 'earlier use of pharmacotherapeutic treatment should be considered' does not say which population it means (C65). LB-6.1 and LB-M4 therefore state only the common ground: standards differ between countries, and not everyone needs medicine. LB-6.T now adds the EDITORIAL line 「要不要用藥，請和醫師討論個人狀況」, so that readers whom the Chinese or Japanese guideline would treat are pointed to a doctor. The fact-check confirmed the ACR direction (S33 Deep Blue PDF, text and Table 1, certainty 'High†') and judged the common-ground wording fair to all three cited positions. Deliberately not used in patient text: ACR's '24 people treated for 3 years to prevent one flare' (C49), ACR's 'also applies with CKD/CVD/stones/hypertension' (C50), the Japanese and Chinese thresholds, and any kidney-protection claim (myth M10 in the evidence file: Japan conditional FOR, certainty B; ACR against including CKD; Korea no recommendation; CKD-FIX and PERL showed no benefit; Cochrane 2017 'very uncertain'). Please read the Taiwan 2018 consensus full text and the APLAR 2021 statements. If you want the page to state a Taiwanese position, it needs a source we can quote.
